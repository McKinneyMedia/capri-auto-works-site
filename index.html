Good to know

Do I need an appointment?

No. We work first come, first served. Walk in during shop hours, or call ahead if you want to check how busy we are.

Is the emissions diagnostic the same as the emissions inspection?

No. The free emissions diagnostic finds out why your check engine light is on or why you failed. The PA emissions inspection is the state test. Call to ask what the diagnostic covers and to confirm inspection pricing.

Can I see used-car inventory online?

Not right now. Inventory changes quickly, so visit the lot at 2005 Hanover Ave or call to ask what's available.

My car failed somewhere else. Can you help?

Yes. Bring the car and the failure report. We'll look at what failed and tell you what it needs before any work starts.

How will I know when my car is ready?

Leave the best number to reach you when you drop off, and ask us for a time estimate. You can call the shop for an update any time during hours.
